Questions

Common questions

Am I already a member?
If you're not sure whether your household is registered or when your membership was last renewed, reach out to the center and we'll check for you. Contact details are on the main site.
Can one membership cover my whole family?
Membership is normally registered per household. If you'd like your family listed together, or you have a question about your situation, please get in touch and we'll help you sort it out.
Does my membership replace donating?
No — they do different jobs. Membership supports the center's day-to-day operations through the year, while donations go to specific campaigns like the new mosque. Many members do both, as they're able.
Is my membership tax-deductible?
Yes. IEC Ezan of Greater Des Moines is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit, and Givebutter will email you a receipt for your records as soon as your payment goes through.
I'd rather pay in person or by check.
That's completely fine. Speak to someone at the center or contact us and we'll take care of your membership that way.
